## Short description

Eulogy plays one life thirty ways, then stages the funeral with a cast that remembers what happened. In the recorded screening, Léa speaks in 7 of 10 lives where Louis asks for help in the first chapter, against 10 of the other 20. The decision ledger, private dialogue and Bruin query make each result traceable to the characters’ choices.
