# Funeral movement

Quaternius made the **Universal Animation Library** and released the Standard animations under CC0. The walk, sit, stand, seated idle, standing idle and speaking clips in this directory are retargeted to the bundled Ready Player Me and Rocketbox rigs. The application places the feet along the aisle and drives the mouth with the actual voice amplitude. [Author and license](https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html) · [Standard source files](https://github.com/J-Ponzo/gltf-universal-animation-library/tree/e24c23cf2a1323488a3faa226ea7ea21f644b73e)

Regenerate from the Standard source with `node scripts/retarget-chapel-motion.mjs /path/to/AnimationLibrary_Godot_Standard.gltf` from the repository root.
