# Chapel performers

Markus Wojcik and the Rocketbox team created these full-body characters. Microsoft distributes the Rocketbox Avatar Library under the MIT License. Matias Volonte and Fang Ma contributed facial expressions. [Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library](https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox/tree/0943055db6ec570bcef9f2c8b41c9e5467c808f9) · [Upstream license](https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox/blob/0943055db6ec570bcef9f2c8b41c9e5467c808f9/LICENSE.md)

| --- | --- |
| `mother.glb` | [Female Adult 09](https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox/tree/0943055db6ec570bcef9f2c8b41c9e5467c808f9/Assets/Avatars/Adults/Female_Adult_09) |
| `father.glb` | [Male Adult 03](https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox/tree/0943055db6ec570bcef9f2c8b41c9e5467c808f9/Assets/Avatars/Adults/Male_Adult_03) |
| `sister.glb` | [Female Adult 14](https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox/tree/0943055db6ec570bcef9f2c8b41c9e5467c808f9/Assets/Avatars/Adults/Female_Adult_14) |
| `friend.glb` | [Male Adult 11](https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox/tree/0943055db6ec570bcef9f2c8b41c9e5467c808f9/Assets/Avatars/Adults/Male_Adult_11) |

The chapel versions convert the original facial FBXs to glTF in meters. Body joints use Ready Player Me names. The materials include the original color, normal and hair transparency textures. The jaw, blinks and seven speech shapes use the source models’ facial deformations.
