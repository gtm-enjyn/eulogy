# Chapel cast
The two locally bundled Ready Player Me rigs are shared across four roles. The chapel changes their seating, pose and animation at runtime.

- `visitor.glb` is the PreviewMesh in the Ready Player Me Unity SDK. The MIT notice is in RPM-LICENSE.txt. [Source](https://github.com/readyplayerme/rpm-unity-sdk-core/tree/main/Samples%7E/QuickStart/PreviewAvatar)
- `mourner.glb` is the unmodified brunette model distributed by met4citizen in TalkingHead, created with Ready Player Me and licensed for noncommercial use under CC BY-NC 4.0.
