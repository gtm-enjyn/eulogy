# Chapel art credits

`nave.glb` is a bespoke, metre-scale Gothic interior assembled for Eulogy. Its pointed arches, ribbed vaults, clustered piers, choir stalls, carved lectern, casket, bier, candlesticks, stonework and tracery were modelled for this scene. The model embeds its surface photographs. The browser reads both assets locally.

| Material or geometry | Creator and adaptation | Rights and source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pew seats and backs, narthex door with forged hinges, altar body and paired candleholders | tevy, *Stone Chapel*, version 2. Selected geometry from the original building was rescaled, repositioned and given materials suited to this interior. | [Asset and CC0 dedication](https://tevy.dev/models/stone-chapel) · [Pinned source GLB](https://tevy.dev/models/stone-chapel/v2/lod0.glb) |
| Wall colour and normal map | Rob Tuytel, *Medieval Blocks 03*. The colour map was desaturated and graded. | [Poly Haven, CC0](https://polyhaven.com/a/medieval_blocks_03) |
| Flagstone colour | Rob Tuytel, *Large Floor Tiles 02*. Resampled and graded. | [Poly Haven, CC0](https://polyhaven.com/a/large_floor_tiles_02) |
| Wood colour, normal and roughness maps | Jenelle van Heerden, *Walnut Veneer*. The maps were resampled. The wood colour was graded for the pews, portal, lectern and casket. | [Poly Haven, CC0](https://polyhaven.com/a/walnut_veneer) |
| Glazing | Paolo Neo, *Cathedral window inside*. Three window details from the choir of the Cathedral of Saints Peter and Paul, Brno, were cropped, rectified and graded. | [Wikimedia Commons, public-domain dedication by the photographer](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cathedral_window_inside.jpg) |
| Interior image-based lighting | Dimitrios Savva, photography, and Jarod Guest, processing, *Chapel Day*. The Radiance panorama was reduced to 512 × 256 pixels in linear light. | [Poly Haven, CC0](https://polyhaven.com/a/chapel_day) |
